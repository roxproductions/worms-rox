Field manual

Move

←→ walk  Enter jump, again in mid-air for a backflip (then steer with ←→)  ↑↓ aim

Fire

Hold Space to charge, release to fire. Dynamite and shotgun fire instantly.

1 Bazooka 2 Grenade 3 Dynamite 4 Shotgun 5 Fire punch 6 Flamethrower 7 Super Sheep 8 Kitty Cannon

Super Sheep: Space launches it, then ←→↑↓ steer and Space detonates. You start with 1 sheep and 2 kitty volleys.

Ninja rope

R shoot a grapple along your aim (press again to let go, then again to chain a new hook). ↑ reel in (you speed up as you shorten the rope), ↓ reel out, ←→ pump the swing, the rope stays on until you press R again, which lets go. Press R once more to fire a new hook.

Camera

Wheel zooms, drag pans, C follow, double-click resets, F fullscreen.

Crates

Press Space while crates are parachuting down to cut the parachute so they drop fast. Orange sudden duel (every team) · Star ★ flamethrower, Super Sheep or Kitty ammo · Red ◎ first-person deathmatch (all teams, UT-style weapons; mouse sensitivity with - / +) · Purple ? a random duel (Rock Paper Scissors, Tetris race, Mortal Kombat, Smash) · Shoot a crate and it explodes, so don't shoot the ones you want. The loser's worm dies (last place in a multi-team duel).

Rules

Turn-based, 30 s each, last team standing wins. With two worms left both players fight at once. Hide in castles, saloons and buildings, shoot from windows.
